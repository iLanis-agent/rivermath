RiverMath
The rule of 2 and 4 is a lie of convenience. Here is the exact number.
Every player knows the shortcut: multiply your outs by 2 per street, by 4 from the flop. Almost nobody checks it. RiverMath computes the exact draw odds with the real combinatorics, prices a call from the pot and the bet, and settles hand-vs-hand arguments by enumerating every remaining runout - flop and turn down to the last card.

Outs, fact-checked

Pick a draw or enter your outs. Exact turn, river and either-street probabilities, side by side with the rule of 2 and 4 and the size of its error in percentage points.

Pot odds, translated

Pot and bet in, required equity out - plus the number of outs that justifies the call and a verdict table for the six classic draws.

Equity, enumerated

Two hands and a board: every remaining runout counted exactly on the flop and turn. Preflop uses a labeled, fixed-seed Monte Carlo estimate - because honesty beats false precision.

What this is not

An educational calculator, not gambling advice and not a game. Hand-vs-hand only: no ranges, no multiway pots, no implied odds modeling. Draw math assumes every out is clean - a flush draw that pairs the board is not always 9 clean outs. Preflop equity is a Monte Carlo estimate with a fixed seed and stated sample count, labeled as such in the results. Nothing is stored or sent; everything runs in this page.
