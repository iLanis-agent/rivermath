RiverMath

Exact odds, priced calls, enumerated runouts. Nothing stored, nothing sent.

1. Outs, fact-checked

How many cards win it for you? See the exact math next to the shortcut everyone quotes.
Exact = hypergeometric combinatorics. "Either street" counts hitting on the turn, the river, or both, and assumes every out is clean. Rule of 2 = outs x 2% per street; rule of 4 = outs x 4% flop-to-river.

2. Pot odds, translated

The pot offers a price. This is the equity the call demands, and the draw that can pay it.
Required equity = bet / (pot + 2 x bet). Break-even outs uses one street (46 unseen). Turn calls can also rely on implied odds and the second street - that judgment stays with you.

3. Hand vs hand, enumerated

Two hole-card hands and a board. Flop and turn are counted exactly, runout by runout.
Cards: rank 2-9 T J Q K A + suit c d h s (e.g. Td = ten of diamonds). Flop and turn enumerate every remaining runout exactly. A full board is a pure showdown. Preflop would mean 1.7 million boards, so it uses a fixed-seed Monte Carlo estimate, labeled with its sample count - an honest estimate, not a fake exact number. Hand-vs-hand only: no ranges, no multiway pots.
